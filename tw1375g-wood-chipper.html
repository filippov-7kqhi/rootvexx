Questions

About the TW 13/75G

What size timber will it take?

Ask us for the rated capacity before you buy — we quote it from the manufacturer's figure rather than from a photograph, and forked or very green material effectively reduces it whatever the rating says.

Do I need an operator qualification?

There is no legal licence to own or run one. For commercial work HSE expects operators to be trained and competent — City & Guilds NPTC 0020 is the recognised UK unit. We supply the manual and a risk-assessment template; we do not certify.

How loud is it, really?

92 dB(A) at the operator position and 120 dB(A) guaranteed sound power, both taken from the machine's own plate. Above 85 dB(A) hearing protection is mandatory under the Control of Noise at Work Regulations 2005, so wear the defenders supplied.

Can I tow it behind a car?

The wheels are for moving it around a site, not for the road. Move it on a trailer or in a van; it is not supplied as a road-legal trailer and has no lighting board.

What does a set of blades cost?

Ask us — we quote blades individually, with the price and the delivery time.
